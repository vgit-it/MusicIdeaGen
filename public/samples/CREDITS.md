# Sample credits

## Piano, acoustic guitar, electric guitar, electric bass
From [tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments) by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky.
Samples released under [CC-BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Changes: subset of notes, trimmed, converted to mono 80 kbps MP3.

## Drums (kick, snare, hi-hat, toms, crash, ride)
From [Big Rusty Drums](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums) by Karoryfer Samples.
Released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain).
Changes: close and overhead mics mixed to mono, trimmed, normalized, converted to MP3.

Regenerate with `npm run samples` (see `scripts/prepare-samples.mjs`).
