What this checks

Whether the voice you set up in the Voice tab holds up across its range. Three things went wrong: low notes came out as one quiet hum (all the energy in the bare note, so they vanish on a phone), "ee" at low and middle pitches came out as a pure, whistling tone, and a falsetto (the pop voice up high) came out bare and hissy, with dips at some notes. The orange line is the voice as it is now; the dashed grey line is the same voice with the low-note, "ee" and falsetto fixes switched off. Nothing here is played until you press a play button.

Checking: press Measure. The voice and singer are shared with the Voice tab, so changes to its sliders are measured too (the fix sliders are under "Low notes, ee and falsetto").

How loud each note sounds

nowfixes off

As the ear hears it (low notes count for less), against the middle of the range. The line should stay near 0. Dips into the shaded area are notes that sound quiet.

How much of each note is the bare tone

nowfixes off

The share of the sound that is only the note's own pitch. A real voice has plenty of overtones; near 100% is a hum or a whistle. The line should stay under the shaded band.

How clean each note is

nowfixes off

How far the tone stands above the hiss and noise between its overtones (dB). Higher is cleaner. A sung note is usually 20 dB or more; a dip into the shaded band is noise you can hear.

One note, harmonic by harmonic

nowfixes off

Each bar is one overtone, from the note itself (1) up to 5 kHz. A voice that carries has bars 2 to 8 standing well up from the floor; a hum has one tall bar.

Hear the whole climb

One held note every three semitones, low to high, on the chosen vowel. Played plain (not the app's mix), so low notes can be judged on a phone speaker or a laptop. Measure first.

Words to sing

One line per phrase. Words come from the CMU pronouncing dictionary (). A word it does not have can be typed as its sounds in braces, e.g. {HH AH0 L OW1} (ARPAbet; 1 after a vowel marks the stress).

Blind quiz: which word was it?

A word is sung, you pick it from words that differ in one sound. The score per contrast shows which sounds are not clear. Tick the box to mix in the app's earlier consonants without telling you which is which, and compare the two columns. Uses the voice, pitch and consonant sliders above.
